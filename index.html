The receipts

Every joke on this page is doing unpaid labour for a real news story. Here are the ones it is carrying.

0 Discs in the disc box

The "physical" edition is reported to be a box with a download code inside. Same shelf, same price, same cover art, no disc. You cannot lend it, resell it, or install it from the cardboard.

2 Shops that said no

Video Games Plus and Loot Box Gaming both refused to stock the biggest launch in the medium's history rather than sell a box of air. Roughly 40 years of goodwill, spent on principle.

1 Legal filing, one phrase

Answering a California suit over "Buy Now," Sony argued a reasonable consumer already knows a digital game is licensed, not sold. The agreement says it plainly: licensed to you, not sold.

2028 When the discs stop

PlayStation disc production is set to wind down by January 2028. The company that spent thirty years selling you a shelf would now like the shelf back.
